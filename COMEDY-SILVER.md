# Comedy Silver

Status: unsorted ore. Not Comedy Gold.

Comedy Silver is where a find waits before anyone pretends it has been
sorted. A line can be funny and still be the wrong size, the wrong cut, or
not yet chosen for the book. Silver is the holding pile. It is not a lesser
metal. The author said the size tiers are real and the substance is not:

> Tiers of size, but remember: it's all gold!! @_@

Justichuu, exact public fragment received on August 29, 2026. Marked Comedy
Silver. Not selected for Comedy Gold.

## The route

1. A find is released on purpose and lands here as Comedy Silver.
2. A miner sorts the pile: keep, cut, or leave.
3. What is gold goes to the smelter. Nuggets and hunks are size names for
   the same metal. The smelter turns them into bars.
4. Bars that Justichuu selects are exported to [Comedy Gold](BOOK.md#comedy-gold)
   in the book, with speaker, wording, source, and date.

No tool may skip the pile and write a find straight into Comedy Gold. No
tool may mine a private conversation into this file. A human has to choose
the exact public text first.

## Size names

| Name | What it is | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nugget | A short kept line | Silver until selected |
| Hunk | A longer kept piece | Silver until selected |
| Bar | A selected, attributed line in the book | [Comedy Gold](BOOK.md#comedy-gold) |

The table is a size list. It is not a quality ranking. It's all gold.
