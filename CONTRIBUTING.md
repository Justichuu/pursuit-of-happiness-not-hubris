# Contributing

## Current status

Pull-request proposals are open for corrections, accessibility work, prose,
quotations, illustrations, and public book tooling. Opening a pull request is a
proposal for review. It is not a promise to merge, publish, employ, or pay.

Before doing substantial work, open a small proposal that names the scope and
one contribution mode:

- `Volunteer correction`: a contributor knowingly offers a limited correction
  without payment.
- `Paid terms required`: the exact amount or formula and payment route must be
  agreed before merge.
- `Discussion only`: no usable creative submission and no payment expectation.

Nobody must choose the volunteer mode to receive review. Please do not perform
substantial speculative work in expectation of a later reward.

## Review and merge gate

Before a paid contribution is merged, its review record must state:

1. The exact revenue pool and whether calculations use gross or net receipts.
2. The eligibility, attribution, split, timing, minimum payout, and tax rules.
3. The license and rights granted by both the contributor and the project.
4. A conflict, removal, correction, and appeal process.
5. A plain statement of what happens if revenue remains zero.
6. A versioned record that cannot retroactively reduce an earned share without
   the affected contributor's agreement.

The maintainer reviews each pull request for editorial fit, rights, privacy,
accessibility, security, and payment terms. Rejection does not transfer the
contributor's copyright or create a debt.

## Publication rules

- Submit only material you have the right and consent to release.
- Do not submit passwords, keys, recovery phrases, addresses, private medical or
  family information, private conversations, or unpublished security details.
- A quotation must preserve exact wording, speaker, source, and visible cuts.
  Comedy finds land in [Comedy Silver](COMEDY-SILVER.md) first. They are not
  Comedy Gold until a miner sorts them and Justichuu selects a bar.
- AI assistance must be disclosed. Generated text is never evidence that a fact
  is true or that a person consented.
- Voices stay separated. In a chapter that declares separated voices, every
  passage carries a voice marker, generated text never appears inside a
  Justichuu block, and a slot reserved for him is left unwritten rather than
  drafted for him. See [VOICES.md](VOICES.md).
- Corrections to a Justichuu passage are proposed in review for him to apply,
  not edited in place by a contributor or a tool.
- Accessibility and plain-language corrections are part of editorial quality.
- No copied characters, trade dress, lyrics, samples, or other protected work.

Use the repository pull-request template. Never put bank details, tax records,
wallet recovery material, or payment credentials in GitHub. ChuuPayMe and
Stripe may be proposed as payment rails under [PAYMENT.md](PAYMENT.md), but a
rail is not itself a payout promise.

The public license cannot guarantee that every reuser pays. The project can be
honest about money it controls and cannot promise money it does not control.
