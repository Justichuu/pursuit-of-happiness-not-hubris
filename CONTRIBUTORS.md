# Contributors

Anyone can be here. There are two ways in and they are worth equal respect.

**A writing voice** may open passages in the book with their own marker.
**An attribution** is a person this work owes something to, who writes nothing.
Being listed as an attribution is a complete way to be in this book, not a
lesser one, and it is the right row for someone who gave an idea, a correction,
a picture, a conversation, or permission to use their work.

## How a row gets here

Open a pull request that adds your row. That is the whole process. The row is
the registration: `validate.py` will not accept a voice marker for a name that
is not on this list, so the list is what makes a voice real rather than a
typo or somebody signing as you.

Nobody is added without their say. If you want your row removed, open a pull
request that removes it, and if that is not something you can do, ask and it
will be done for you. A name here is not a permanent claim on you.

## What a name has to look like

Letters, digits, spaces, hyphens, full stops and apostrophes. Between one and
forty characters. It must not contain an email address, a link, or a handle
belonging to a service that could go away and take the meaning with it.

Use the name you want read aloud. A pseudonym is fine. Your legal name is not
required anywhere in this book, and if you give one it will be used exactly as
you wrote it and nowhere else.

## What a writing voice may do

Open a passage with

```text
**Voice: Your Name.**
```

inside a chapter that has declared `**Voices: separated.**`, and write under it.
Nobody edits inside anybody else's passage. A correction to another person's
text is a note in your own voice, or a pull request they accept, never a quiet
rewrite. See [VOICES.md](VOICES.md).

The reserved slot, `_Unwritten. Justichuu writes here._`, stays his alone. It is
the one place the rule does not open, because its whole purpose is to hold a
space that nobody else fills in.

## What an attribution may do

Nothing, and that is the point. An attribution row is a record that this work
is not only its authors. It carries no obligation to write and no expectation
that you ever will.

If you are here as an attribution and later want to write, change your own row
to a writing voice in a pull request. If you are here as a writing voice and
want to stop, change it back. Neither direction needs a reason.
