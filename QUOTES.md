# Quotes

Exact words somebody said, kept exactly. Anyone on
[CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md) can add one.

This file holds the same discipline Comedy Gold already holds, because a
quotation is the one kind of text where being close is the same as being wrong.
Reproduce spelling, capitalisation and punctuation as they were. If you skip
material inside a quotation, mark the gap with a spaced ellipsis, ` … `, and
never use it to join words in a way that reverses or changes what the speaker
meant. If the result needs explaining to be fair, it is not ready.

A quote here is a record, not an endorsement. Keeping somebody's words accurate
is a courtesy owed even to people you disagree with, and especially to them.

## What you may not put here

- Anything said to you in confidence, or anywhere a reasonable person expected
  not to be quoted.
- A private individual's words used to identify or embarrass them.
- Anything you did not personally verify against the source. An unverified
  quotation is a rumour with quotation marks on it.
- A paraphrase wearing quotation marks. Write it as your own sentence instead.

If the speaker asks to be removed, they are removed. No case is made and no
reason is required.

## The shape of an entry

Copy this block, fill it in, and add it at the end. The identifier is the next
number in sequence.

```text
## Q-0001

> The exact words, reproduced exactly.

- speaker: the name to be read aloud
- source: where it was said, specifically enough to find
- said: 2026-01-01
- selected: 2026-01-01
- added by: a name from CONTRIBUTORS.md
```

`said` is when the speaker said it. `selected` is when somebody chose to put it
here. They are different facts and conflating them hides who made the choice.

Use `unknown` for a field you cannot establish. That is an honest answer and it
is better than a confident guess. Do not delete the field.
